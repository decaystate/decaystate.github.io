Follow DECAYSTATE

Heavy, deliberate metalcore out of Sioux Falls, South Dakota: corroded low end and sharp, mechanical breakdowns, with hooks that stick.

More about the band

DECAYSTATE formed in early 2026 from seasoned musicians in the Sioux Falls scene: Nick on vocals, Paul on guitar and Alex on drums. Since their first show at Icon in September, they’ve played multiple dates, including support slots with Mirrorcell and Opal in Sky.

The singles Hollow and Bones are out now, a first look at where the band is headed, both sonically and visually. A cover of Faouzia’s Unethical followed in October, with more to come before the end of the year.

Live, the band runs its own in-ear monitor rig and brings 15+ years of live experience to every set.
